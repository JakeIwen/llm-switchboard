Sessions search · Filters the list as you type. Every term must match; case doesn't matter.
  • word in the title, first prompt, folder, id or Remote Control name; "two words" for a phrase
  • -word excludes; works with everything below too, e.g. -dir:tmp
  • dir:obd folder · title:telem · id:c969
  • is:kept is:off is:running is:open is:busy is:attention is:elsewhere is:claude is:codex
  • age:<2d active in the last 2 days · age:>1w older than a week (units m h d w)
  • state:backoff exact supervisor state
  • Enter searches the same words inside transcripts. Session-only filters are left out; Esc restores this query.
Transcripts search · Searches prompts and visible replies. Press Enter to run.
  • Several words are all required; "two words" is a phrase; -word excludes
  • tool:claude or tool:codex · dir:obd
  • by:me your prompts · by:reply visible replies (thinking and tool output aren't indexed)
  • age:<2d / age:>1w · after:2026-10-01 · before:2026-10-18
  • Include a word or phrase, not only filters. The session status, tool and folder controls don't apply here.
  • Esc, clearing the query, or choosing Sessions restores your earlier session query.
